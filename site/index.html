Setup

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Utter is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. Drag it to Applications and double-click.

Where do I get an API key?

Create a free key at console.groq.com/keys. No card needed. Paste it into Utter’s Settings.

Which permissions does it need?

Microphone, to hear you, and Accessibility, so it can paste into other apps. Turn on Accessibility under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Utter lives in your menu bar, and shows in the Dock while its window is open.

What does it cost?

Nothing for personal use. Groq’s free tier covers around 2,000 dictations a day. Past that, Groq charges about $0.04 per hour of audio.

Where does my audio go?

Recordings stay on your Mac and are sent to Groq only to be transcribed and cleaned up. To spell names right, Utter can include the text already in the box you’re typing in; it’s never stored, and you can turn it off. You choose how long recordings are kept and can delete any one from History.
